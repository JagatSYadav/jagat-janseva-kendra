How to Use on Phone & Computer

Step 1: Install as an App on Android Phone (Chrome)

  1. Open https://jagatsyadav.github.io/jagat-janseva-kendra/ in Google Chrome on Owner Phone & Staff Phone.
  2. Tap the 3 Dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  3. Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  4. An icon for JAGAT JAN SEVA KENDRA will appear on your home screen like a real app.

Step 2: Login Credentials & Backup

  • Center ID: JJSK-MAIN-01
  • Owner logs in with Owner PIN (default 1234).
  • Shop Assistant logs in with Staff PIN (default 0000).
  • Use JSON Backup and Restore JSON to transfer or back up data between devices anytime.
